## Route

### Ironforge

1. 📍 Ironforge 50.8, 5.6
    - 💬 Talk to **Gerrig Bonegrip**
    - ⚠️ Skip this if you are not near Ironforge, and ask your group to share Knowledge in the Deeps instead
    - ❗ Accept [Knowledge in the Deeps](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=971)
2. 📍 Ironforge
    - 💬 Talk to **Gryth Thurden**
    - ✈️ Fly to Menethil Harbor
3. 📍 Wetlands 4.4, 56.8
    - 🚶 Take the boat to Auberdine in Darkshore

### Auberdine

4. 📍 Darkshore 38.3, 43.0
    - 💬 Talk to **Gershala Nightwhisper**
    - ⚠️ If you skip this, ask your group to share it with you
    - ❗ Accept [Researching the Corruption](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=1275)
5. *Optional* · 📍 Darkshore
    - 💬 Talk to **Innkeeper Shaussiy** downstairs
    - 🏠 Set your Hearthstone to Auberdine
6. 📍 Darkshore
    - 💬 Talk to **Caylais Moonfeather**
    - ✈️ Fly to Rut'theran Village

### Darnassus

7. 📍 Darnassus 55.2, 24.0
    - 💬 Take the portal up to Darnassus and go upstairs. Talk to **Argent Guard Manados**
    - ⚠️ If you skip this, ask your group to share it with you
    - ❗ Accept [Twilight Falls](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=1199)
8. 📍 Darnassus 55.4, 25.0
    - 💬 Talk to **Dawnwatcher Shaedlass** up stairs
    - ⚠️ If you skip this, ask your group to share it with you
    - ❗ Accept [In Search of Thaelrid](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=1198)
9. 📍 Teldrassil
    - 💬 Take the portal down to Rut'theran Village. Talk to **Vesprystus**
    - ✈️ Fly to Auberdine

### Blackfathom Deeps

10. 📍 Ashenvale 14.2, 14.6
    - Run south along the coast of Darkshore into Ashenvale, to the Zoram Strand. Find a group
11. 🔁 *Along the way*
    - Kill **Fallenroot Rogues**, **Fallenroot Satyrs**, **Blackfathom Oracles** and **Blackfathom Tide Priestesses**. Loot them for their **Corrupted Brain Stems**
    - ⚠️ You can also loot them inside the dungeon
    - 🎯 Corrupted Brain Stem (8) — [Researching the Corruption](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=1275)
12. 🚶 Enter Blackfathom Deeps
13. 🔁 *Along the way*
    - Kill **Nagas** and **Satyrs**. Loot them for their **Corrupted Brain Stems**
    - 🎯 Corrupted Brain Stem (8) — [Researching the Corruption](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=1275)
14. 🔁 *Along the way*
    - ⚠️ If you loot a **Blackfathom Pearl**, use it to start The Heart of the Void
    - ❗ Accept [The Heart of the Void](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=78916)
15. Open the **Pitted Iron Chest** underwater near the area with the turtles. Loot it for **Lorgalis' Manuscript**
    - 🎯 Lorgalis Manuscript (1) — [Knowledge in the Deeps](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=971)
    - *Only if you have [Knowledge in the Deeps](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=971)*
16. 💬 Talk to **Argent Guard Thaelrid**
    - ⚠️ You can accept Blackfathom Villainy without In Search of Thaelrid, or ask your group to share it
    - ✅ Turn in [In Search of Thaelrid](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=1198)
    - ❗ Accept [Blackfathom Villainy](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=1200)
17. 🔁 *Along the way*
    - Kill the **Twilight's Hammer**. Loot them for their **Twilight Pendants**
    - 🎯 Twilight Pendant (10) — [Twilight Falls](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=1199)
18. Kill **Twilight Lord Kelris**. Loot him for his **Head**
    - 🎯 Head of Kelris (1) — [Blackfathom Villainy](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=1200)
19. Finish looting **Twilight Pendants** and **Corrupted Brain Stems** before you leave
    - ⚠️ The altar at the end of the dungeon teleports you back to the entrance. The mobs outside also drop Corrupted Brain Stems
    - 🎯 Twilight Pendant (10) — [Twilight Falls](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=1199)
    - 🎯 Corrupted Brain Stem (8) — [Researching the Corruption](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=1275)
