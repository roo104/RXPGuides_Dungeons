## Route

### Darnassus and Moonglade pickups

1. 📍 Darnassus
    - 💬 Talk to **Mathrengyl Bearwalker** on the middle level of the Cenarion Enclave in Darnassus
    - ❗ Accept [Lessons Anew](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=6121)
    - ❗ Accept [A Lesson to Learn](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=26)
2. 🔁 *Along the way*
    - ✨ Cast [Teleport: Moonglade](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/spell=18960)
3. 📍 Moonglade
    - 💬 Talk to **Dendrite Starblaze** up stairs
    - ✅ Turn in [Lessons Anew](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=6121)
    - ❗ Accept [The Principal Source](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=6122)
    - ✅ Turn in [A Lesson to Learn](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=26)
    - ❗ Accept [Trial of the Lake](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=29)
4. 📍 Moonglade
    - Swim into Lake Elune'ara
    - Open a **Bauble Container**. Loot it for a [Shrine Bauble](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=15877)
    - ⚠️ It may spawn in different locations underwater
    - 🎒 Collect [Shrine Bauble](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=15877) (1)
5. 📍 Moonglade
    - ⚠️ Use the [Shrine Bauble](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=15877) at the Shrine of Remulos tree
    - 🎯 Complete the Trial of the Lake — [Trial of the Lake](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=29)
6. 📍 Moonglade
    - 💬 Talk to **Tajarri**
    - ✅ Turn in [Trial of the Lake](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=29)
    - ❗ Accept [Trial of the Sea Lion](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=272)

### Darkshore

7. 📍 Moonglade
    - 💬 Talk to **Silva Fil'naveth**
    - ✈️ Fly to Rut'theran Village
8. 📍 Teldrassil
    - 💬 Talk to **Vesprystus**
    - ✈️ Fly to Darkshore
9. 📍 Darkshore
    - ⚠️ Use the **Empty Cliffspring Falls Sampler** in the water at the entrance of the Cliffspring River Cave
    - 🎯 Filled Cliffspring Falls Sampler (1) — [The Principal Source](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=6122)
10. 📍 Darkshore
    - ⚠️ Swim out in the water
    - Open the **Strange Lockbox**. Loot it for the [Half Pendant of Aquatic Agility](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=15883)
    - 🎒 Collect [Half Pendant of Aquatic Agility](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=15883) (1)
11. 📍 Darkshore
    - 💬 Talk to **Alanndarian Nightsong** in Auberdine
    - ✅ Turn in [The Principal Source](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=6122)
    - ❗ Accept [Gathering the Cure](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=6123)
12. 🔁 *Along the way*
    - Get 5 **Earthroot**
    - ⚠️ Pick it with Herbalism (skill 15), find it in **Battered Chests**, or buy it from the Auction House
    - 🎯 Earthroot (5) — [Gathering the Cure](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=6123)
13. 📍 Darkshore
    - Loot **Lunar Fungus** on the ground throughout the caves
    - 🎯 Lunar Fungus (12) — [Gathering the Cure](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=6123)
14. Finish getting 5 **Earthroot**
    - ⚠️ Pick it with Herbalism (skill 15), find it in **Battered Chests**, or buy it from the Auction House
    - 🎯 Earthroot (5) — [Gathering the Cure](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=6123)
15. 📍 Darkshore
    - 💬 Talk to **Alanndarian Nightsong**
    - ✅ Turn in [Gathering the Cure](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=6123)
    - ❗ Accept [Curing the Sick](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=6124)
16. 📍 Darkshore
    - ⚠️ Use the [Curative Animal Salve](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=15826) on **Sickly Deer**
    - ⚠️ They are spread from the Cliffspring River down to the Ashenvale border
    - 🎯 Sickly Deer cured (10) — [Curing the Sick](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=6124)

### Westfall

17. 📍 Darkshore 30.5, 40.9
    - 🚶 Take the boat from Auberdine to Stormwind
18. 📍 Stormwind City
    - 💬 Talk to **Dungar Longdrink**
    - ⚠️ If you do not have the Westfall flight path, run south through Elwynn Forest instead
    - ✈️ Fly to Westfall
19. 📍 Westfall 17.9, 33.1
    - ⚠️ Swim out to sea off the west coast of Westfall
    - Open the **Strange Lockbox** deep underwater. Loot it for the [Half Pendant of Aquatic Endurance](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=15882)
    - 🎒 Collect [Half Pendant of Aquatic Endurance](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=15882) (1)
