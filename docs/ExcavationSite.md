## Route

📍 location · 💬 talk · ❗ accept · 🎯 objective · 🎒 collect · ✅ turn in · 🔁 do alongside the next steps

### Lakeshire

1. *Optional* · 📍 Redridge Mountains
    - 💬 Talk to **Dorin Songblade** in Lakeshire
    - ⚠️ Skip this if you are not in Redridge Mountains
    - ❗ Accept [Songblade Search](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=95772)
2. 📍 Redridge Mountains
    - 💬 Talk to **Ariena Stormfeather**
    - ⚠️ If you do not have the Menethil Harbor flight path, fly to Thelsamar in Loch Modan instead. It is much faster than running from Ironforge
    - ⚠️ If you do not have the Thelsamar flight path either, fly to Stormwind, take the Deeprun Tram to Ironforge and run east through Dun Morogh and Loch Modan. Get the Thelsamar flight path on the way
    - ⚠️ Or take the boats: from Stormwind harbor to Auberdine in Darkshore, then from Auberdine to Menethil Harbor. No flight paths needed, but you have to wait for two boats
    - ✈️ Fly to Menethil Harbor
3. 🔁 *Along the way* · 📍 Wetlands
    - 🚶 Run north from Loch Modan through Dun Algaz into the Wetlands, then follow the road west to Menethil Harbor
